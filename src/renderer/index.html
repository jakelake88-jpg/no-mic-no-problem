Bluetooth mode (experimental)

No Wi-Fi, no cable? Your phone can stream over Bluetooth like a speaker. Expect 0.1–0.25 s delay (a Bluetooth limit) and note that all phone sounds go into the mic — silence notifications first.

  1. Pair the phone with this PC (Windows Settings → Bluetooth).
  2. Select the phone below and click Connect.
  3. One-time: route the helper's audio to the virtual mic — open Sound settings, find Windows PowerShell under App volume, set its Output to CABLE Input.
  4. On the phone page, tap Bluetooth mode, then the mic button.
